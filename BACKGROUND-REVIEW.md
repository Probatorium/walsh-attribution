# Background review

**Status: not started.**

This file exists so that the absence of a background review is a recorded
state rather than an omission a reader has to notice.

## What follows from it being unstarted

Until this review is opened and closed, this repository asserts the novelty
of nothing. No claim of novelty, priority, first result, or "not previously
reported" may appear in any artefact of this lane. That prohibition is
written into `PREREGISTRATION.md` section (g) and is not weakened by
anything found in a later session.

The term gate is not a background review and does not stand in for one. It
asked one narrow question about a name, with declared queries and declared
limits, and it is recorded in `NAME-GATE.md`. That it returned a definite
answer about the name says nothing about the field.

## What this review will have to cover when it is opened

Recorded now, before the measurement, so that the scope is not shaped by what
the measurement turns out to be.

1. Spectral analysis of the King Wen sequence, or of any hexagram ordering,
   in any basis. What has been computed, by whom, and under what convention.
2. Conditional or constrained permutation nulls applied to a sequence with a
   known pairing structure, in any field. The non exchangeable null model
   literature is the obvious place and is not the only one.
3. Attribution of an observed excess between a marginal null and a
   conditional null: whether the decomposition fixed in
   `PREREGISTRATION.md` section (d) has a standard name, and if so, what
   that literature says about its failure modes.
4. Whether the exact facts derived by hand in section (b), in particular the
   invariance of the interaction order profile under the four bit
   conventions and the exact order 6 mean under the pair preserving family,
   are already known. They are elementary, so the prior probability that they
   are new is low, and this lane will not claim them until this review says
   otherwise.

Point four is the one most likely to be skipped by an author in a hurry, and
it is written down here for that reason.
