# References

Every work this lane cites, with what it is used for and whether this
repository has verified anything about it. A reference here is a promise that
a reader can obtain the thing and check it.

Three rules govern this list and are enforced elsewhere:

- Nothing without a deposit or a public archive appears here. The closed lane
  whose apparatus this one reuses is therefore absent by design, and its
  reuse is recorded in `PROVENANCE.md` instead. See `CONTACT-RULES.md` rule
  two.
- The repositories of contact rule five may never be named in this file. The
  untouchable gate refuses that separately and loudly, because naming a
  source here is citing it.
- A figure taken from any work below is cited-unverified in `FIGURES.jsonl`
  until an artefact of this repository produces it.

  section; the statement of the pair rule quoted in section (d); and the
  received King Wen sequence, which has not yet entered this repository.
- **Read at**: tag `zenodo-v3` of `kingwen-orderings-replication`, under

- **Relation**: same author as this lane. This is a self audit and
  `PREREGISTRATION.md` section 0 declares it.
